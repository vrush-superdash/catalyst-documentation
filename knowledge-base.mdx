---
title: "Knowledge Base"
---

A Knowledge Base stores information about your business, such as events, menus, operating hours, promotions, facilities, and other important details.

## 1. Open Knowledge Bases

1. From the left sidebar, click **Knowledge Bases**.
2. You will see the list of existing Knowledge Bases.
3. Click **Create Knowledge Base** in the top-right corner to create a new one.

Once created, open the Knowledge Base to start adding information.

## 2. Adding Information to a Knowledge Base

You can add information to a Knowledge Base using four different methods:

- Paragraphs: Add information directly as text.
- Sheets: Add structured information using spreadsheet data.
- Weblinks: Add information by providing website links.
- Files: Upload documents containing information you want the assistant to use.

### Adding Information Using Paragraphs

Use paragraphs when you want to manually add information about your business, such as menus, events, promotions, operating hours, or restaurant facilities.

**You can add up to 30 text blocks in one entry. Each text block supports up to 400 words or 2,500 characters, whichever comes first. Use Add more to include additional text blocks.**

Example: 

```text
### Restaurant Overview
City Line Bar & Grill is a lively, neighborhood-focused restaurant serving approachable American fare, creative cocktails, and a welcoming atmosphere. Its fun and energetic vibe makes it a great place for casual dinners and nights out with friends.

### Happy Hour
Happy hour is available Monday through Friday from 3 PM to 6 PM.

### Weekend Brunch
Weekend brunch is available every Saturday and Sunday from 11 AM to 2 PM.
```

Keep related information together so it is easier to maintain and update.

You can add multiple paragraphs to cover different topics.

## 3. Connect the Knowledge Base to an Assistant

After creating and populating your Knowledge Base:

1. Go to **Assistants** from the sidebar.
2. Open the assistant you want to configure.
3. Navigate to **Knowledge Base** under the Configure section.
4. Select the Knowledge Base you created.

An assistant can have a maximum of **3 Knowledge Bases** connected at a time.
